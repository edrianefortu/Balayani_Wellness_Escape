WHY BALAYANI?

A complete Mindoro reset, without the planning stress.

Balayani combines convenient travel, restful stays, local food, gentle movement, and meaningful community in one carefully planned journey.

Our target guests are students, professionals, and couples looking for a restorative coastal escape with nature, wellness, and connection.

TRAVEL GUIDE

Plan with confidence.

☀ Best for

A short 3-day wellness reset for students, professionals, and couples.

🎒 What to bring

Comfortable clothes, sun protection, reusable water bottle, personal medicines, and practical beach/nature gear.

🌦 Weather & schedule

Activity sequence may shift for ferry schedules, weather, tides, and supplier availability.

♻ Responsible travel

Respect local communities, keep coastal and nature areas clean, minimize waste, and follow guide and safety instructions.

🧭 Safety

Nature activities are subject to safety and access conditions. Basic first-aid support is included; travel insurance and medical expenses are not included.
